Meet LIWA

Navigation, with more light.

We’re exploring how technology can help people make more informed travel decisions through:

Navigation
Understand your options.
Safety Context
See information that may matter to your journey.
Community Knowledge
Learn from people who know the places they move through.
Privacy & Control
Stay in control of your information and what you share.
Emergency Support
Access help when you choose to ask for it.
Responsible Technology
Use technology to support judgment—not replace it.

The big idea

We don’t want to decide for you.

We want you to see more.

Because better information gives people more room to choose.
